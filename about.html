About AI NAKSHA STUDIO AND BUILDERS

Ideas become stronger when design and building speak the same language.

AI NAKSHA STUDIO AND BUILDERS is a creative architecture and construction studio led by Wasim Khan in Gharsisar, Bikaner. We combine architectural thinking, 2D drafting, 3D technology, AI-assisted workflows and on-ground construction services.

Our goal is simple: make the project clear before it is built, then carry that clarity through the real space — from floor plan and façade to POP, putty, false ceiling and final finishing.

Wasim Khan
Founder / Creative Lead
Our approach

Less noise.

We prioritize proportion, material, light, usability and construction reality. Every drawing and image should answer a question, not simply look impressive.
